ArcChess is a personal chess training companion. Users link their Chess.com or Lichess account to import games. The app analyzes games with Stockfish WASM to find mistakes.

Three pillars:
1. **The Forge** — Daily 5-minute warmup. 3 diagnostic puzzles + 5 practice puzzles based on recent mistakes. Ends with "Go Battle" button to play rated games. Tracks warmup streaks.
2. **Campaigns** — Skill trees with three branches: Tactics, Positional Play, Endgames. Users earn XP and unlock levels based on real game mistakes.
3. **The Oracle** — Endgame mastery. Practice solved positions. Two modes: Defender (hold the draw) and Converter (finish precisely). Progressively harder positions.

Games feed all three: analysis finds weak spots → Forge warms up today's problem → Campaigns suggests long-term fix → Oracle builds technique.

Old opening study system (Bookshelf, StudyHub, Garden) has been removed.
